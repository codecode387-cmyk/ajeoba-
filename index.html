ABOUT AJEOBA ELEVATORS LIMITED • ABUJA

Setting the Gold Standard in Vertical Mobility & Engineering Precision

AJEOBA ELEVATORS LIMITED is a premier elevator engineering company headquartered in Abuja, Nigeria. We specialize in the complete lifecycle of passenger, panoramic, freight, hospital, and residential elevators — from bespoke manufacturing and structural installation to round-the-clock maintenance, modernization, and authentic replacement parts.

Founded on uncompromised European safety protocols and Japanese mechanical tolerances, our Abuja-based facility maintains West Africa’s most comprehensive stockpile of certified OEM parts and dedicated engineering squads ready for instant deployment.
