About Ajeoba Elevators • Abuja

Setting the Gold Standard in Vertical Mobility

AJEOBA ELEVATORS LIMITED is a premier elevator engineering company headquartered in Abuja, Nigeria. We specialize in the complete lifecycle of passenger, panoramic, freight, hospital, and residential elevators — from bespoke manufacturing to round-the-clock maintenance and authentic replacement parts.

Founded on European safety protocols and Japanese mechanical tolerances, our Abuja facility maintains West Africa's most comprehensive stockpile of certified OEM parts.
